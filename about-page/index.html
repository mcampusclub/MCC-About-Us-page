01 / OUR STORY

The story
behind MCC.

18 MARCH 2008
Where the journey began
The Microsoft Student Partners (MSP) program encouraged students who were passionate about technology, eager to share their knowledge, and ready to develop skills beyond their classroom learning.

As part of the Microsoft Student Partner Program, Microsoft Student Partners from individual colleges established and ran Microsoft Campus Clubs within their respective institutions.

The Microsoft Campus Club was started on 18 March 2008 to facilitate education and knowledge exchange among students interested in Microsoft technologies.

MCC provides a space where students can learn about technology, connect with peers, exchange ideas, and build a community around shared interests. Microsoft Campus Clubs are supported by Microsoft.
